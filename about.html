About Safe Rent

Safe Rent helps students in Kampala find safe, affordable houses without being scammed.

Our Mission

No student should lose money to fake landlords. Every house on Safe Rent is checked before it is listed.

How It Works

  1. Search for a house in your area
  2. Check if it is Verified ✓
  3. Visit the house first
  4. Contact the landlord and pay with receipt

Stay Safe

⚠️ Never pay inspection fees or deposits before seeing the house.

⚠️ Always ask for a receipt.

⚠️ If someone asks you to pay fast, report them.
